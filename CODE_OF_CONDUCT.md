# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full, authoritative
text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
This file summarises it and states how it is enforced here.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their:

- age, body size, visible or invisible disability, or ethnicity;
- sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, or sexual identity and
  orientation;
- level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
  appearance, race, caste, colour or religion.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull
requests and discussions. It also applies when someone officially represents the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer,
[@ngcex](https://github.com/ngcex) on GitHub. For example:

- use GitHub's "Report content" function on the comment or issue; or
- open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel, without details.

Every complaint will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer
respects the privacy and security of the reporter.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 Enforcement Guidelines. In order of
severity, the steps are:

1. correction;
2. warning;
3. temporary ban;
4. permanent ban.
